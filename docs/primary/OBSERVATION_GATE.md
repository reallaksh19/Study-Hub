# Supervised real-child observation gate

This gate runs after the first Math/Teacher Runtime/English vertical slices and before broad curriculum or Grade-5 scale-up.

It is a small usability/learning-loop observation, not a research study.

## Observe

- Does the child rush through paper/Study-Hub just to reach the QR?
- Does the child return willingly from Kani to the independent task?
- Does the game clarify the target concept or merely entertain?
- Do hints restart thinking or reveal the answer too quickly?
- Do stars, streaks or timing increase careless responding?
- Is the activity length appropriate?
- Can the child follow the instructions without adult translation?
- Does the independent return task show useful evidence?
- Does a later retrieval check survive after several days?

## Privacy / evidence rule

Use guardian-supervised observation with minimal necessary data. Do not store medical/psychological labels or infer durable traits from one session.

## Scale gate

Do not use one child's outcome as population evidence. The purpose is to falsify obvious interaction/design assumptions before scaling the architecture.
